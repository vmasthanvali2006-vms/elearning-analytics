About This Project

The E-Learning Course Analytics System uses Python, Flask, MySQL, Pandas, NumPy and data visualisation to summarise course enrolments, completion rates and recorded learning activities.

Completion rate = completed eligible enrolments divided by all eligible enrolments, multiplied by 100. Cancelled enrolments are excluded.
